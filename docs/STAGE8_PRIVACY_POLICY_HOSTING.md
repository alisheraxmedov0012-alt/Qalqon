## 1. Final Privacy Policy text

**QALQON — Privacy Policy**

_Last updated: [DATE]_

Qalqon ("the app", "we") is an on-device parental-control application for Android
(package `uz.faceguard.app`). This policy explains what data the app handles and how.
Contact: [SUPPORT EMAIL]. Developer: [DEVELOPER / LEGAL ENTITY, ADDRESS].

### 1. Summary
- Qalqon works **fully offline**. It has **no Internet permission** and cannot
  transmit your data over the network.
- All data (parent profile, child profiles, face templates, settings, activity log)
  is stored **only on your device**.
- Face processing happens **entirely on the device**. Face templates are **never
  transmitted off the device** and are stored **encrypted**.
- You can **delete your account and all associated data in the app at any time**
  (and via a web resource, see §9).

### 2. Data we handle and why
| Data | Where it comes from | Where it is stored | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Parent name, phone number | You type it at registration | Device-local database (Room) | Identify the account and the parent |
| PIN | You set it | Device-local, stored only as a salted PBKDF2 hash (never the raw PIN) | Unlock the parent controls |
| Camera frames | Your device camera | **Memory only, transient** (per frame) | Detect a face to recognise parent/child |
| Face template (embedding) | Derived on-device from a detected face | Device-local database, **encrypted (AES-256-GCM, Android Keystore)** | Recognise the enrolled parent/child face |
| Child profile (name, restriction level) | You type it | Device-local database | Apply parental rules to the child |
| Protected-app selection | You choose apps | Device-local database | Decide which apps are protected |
| Foreground app (package name) | Usage Access / Accessibility service | Memory / device-local logs | Detect when a protected app is open |
| Activity log (protection events) | Generated by the app | Device-local database | Show the parent what happened |
| Settings (limits, schedules, eye-safety) | You configure them | Device-local DataStore | Apply your rules |
| Subscription status | Google Play Billing | Device-local cache | Show your plan; Play holds the purchase |

### 3. Face and biometric data (children and parents)
- The camera is used only to detect a face and compute a **face template** (an
  embedding) for parent/child recognition. **Raw camera frames are processed in
  memory and are not written to disk.**
- A face template is stored **only on your device**, **encrypted** with a key held in
  the Android Keystore (device-bound). It is **never sent to us or any third party**.
- You can delete a parent or child face template at any time, and deleting the
  account deletes the template and its encryption key.
- Child face data is enrolled and controlled by the **parent/guardian**; the child
  has no account of their own.

### 4. On-device processing — not transmitted off-device
"On-device" means face detection, embedding and matching run locally using models
bundled in the app (ML Kit face detection; TensorFlow Lite MobileFaceNet). Because the
app has no Internet permission, **no face or other personal data is transmitted off
the device**.

### 5. Permissions and why they are needed
- **Camera** — to detect a face for recognition.
- **Usage Access (Usage Stats)** — to know which app is in the foreground, so a
  protected app can be detected. Used only for protection.
- **Display over other apps (Overlay)** — to show the blocking screen over a
  protected app.
- **Accessibility service** — to detect protected-app window transitions and to draw
  the touchable blocking overlay. The service reads **only the foreground package
  name**; it is configured with `canRetrieveWindowContent=false`, so **screen text,
  passwords and notifications are never read**. Enabling it requires a clear in-app
  disclosure and your explicit consent.
- **Notifications** — to show the ongoing protection status and parent alerts.
- **Receive boot completed** — to restore protection after a restart.
- **Biometric/Fingerprint** — for the optional OS unlock of the parent UI.

### 6. Data sharing
**None.** The app does not share personal or sensitive user data with third parties.
It contains no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs. The only external
interaction is **Google Play Billing**, which processes the subscription purchase
handled by Google Play (see §7); the app itself does not transmit your data.

### 7. Subscriptions and Google Play Billing
The optional premium subscription is sold through Google Play. Purchase processing is
handled by **Google Play Billing**; payment is handled by Google Play under Google's
terms and privacy policy. The app stores only a local entitlement/status cache (no
card or payment details).

### 8. Security and retention
- Data is protected by the Android app sandbox; face templates additionally by
  AES-256-GCM encryption with a Keystore key.
- Data is retained on your device until you delete it.

### 9. Deletion (your rights)
- **In-app:** open the app's Privacy settings and choose delete account / delete all
  data. This deletes your account and all associated data (profiles, face templates
  and their encryption key, settings, session and activity log) from the device.
- **External (web):** you may also request deletion by visiting [ACCOUNT DELETION
  URL] (see the hosting requirements). Provide the phone number of your account so we
  can process the request. Because Qalqon stores data only on your device, deleting
  the app or the in-app deletion removes your data; the web resource records and
  confirms deletion requests.
- **Retention:** no data is retained after deletion; we do not keep server copies
  (there is no server/account backend).

### 10. Children's privacy
Qalqon is a **parental-control tool for parents/guardians**; it is not directed at
children and the child does not use the app independently. Child information
(including the child's face template) is entered and controlled by the parent and
stays on the device.

### 11. Changes to this policy
We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date will change and the current version
will always be available at [PRIVACY POLICY URL].

### 12. Contact
Questions or requests: [SUPPORT EMAIL].
