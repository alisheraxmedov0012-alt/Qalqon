## 2. Recommended screenshot sequence (portrait, per locale)
1. Welcome/onboarding — the core promise (parent/child recognition; offline; on-device).
2. Home dashboard — protection status.
3. Children — child profile + "face enrolled" state.
4. Face enrollment — guidance ("Look at the camera").
5. Protected apps — selection list.
6. Rules — per-app policy.
7. Screen time / Schedule / Eye safety — one combined or two shots.
8. Subscription — trial + price + manage.

## 3. What each screenshot must demonstrate (real functionality only)
- Real, reachable screens with **real** (non-fake) data.
- The protection value proposition is legible without reading the description.

## 5. What must NEVER appear
- Fake functionality or unreleased features.
- Fake subscription **price** (prices are dynamic; do not hardcode "$X" in a screenshot —
  use "3-day free trial" wording or a Play-supplied price rendered at capture time).
- Fake reviews, fake statistics, fake AI-accuracy numbers.
- Misleading security claims ("spoof-proof", "unbreakable", "100% secure").
- Fake device compatibility claims.
- Any real personal data of a real child/parent.

## 6. Feature graphic

parental control"), no unsupported claims.
