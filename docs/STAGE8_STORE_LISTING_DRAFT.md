## Forbidden claims (must NOT appear)
"100% secure", "impossible to bypass", "unbreakable", "perfect face recognition",
"guaranteed child detection", "anti-spoofing", "photo/video-proof", "works on every
device", "never fails", "guaranteed privacy", "Google-approved".

## Allowed, factual claims
On-device face recognition for parent/child; fully offline; data stays on the phone;
blocks selected apps for the child; parent/child recognition; parental controls; a
premium subscription with a 3-day free trial (cancellable).

## English
- **App name:** Qalqon — Parental Control
- **Short description:** Parental-control app that keeps children safe inside selected apps
- **Full description:** Qalqon is a face-recognition parental-control app for parents. When your child opens a protected app, Qalqon detects it and applies your parental rules. Face recognition runs entirely on the device; data stays on the phone and is never sent anywhere.
  Features: block selected apps; separate parent/child face recognition; per-app rules, schedules, screen-time limits and eye-safety (premium); extra-time requests; activity history (premium). The accessibility service learns only which app is on screen (package name) — screen text is never read. Premium starts with a 3-day free trial (cancel anytime).
  Note: Qalqon works fully offline. Some system/OEM settings (e.g. battery optimisation) may affect protection.
