Delete your QALQON account

QALQON · v1.0 · Effective date: 7 October 2026

This page explains how to delete your QALQON account and the data associated with it. QALQON is an on-device app: your data is stored on your device, and there is no server copy of it to delete.

1. What QALQON stores

QALQON stores, on your device only: your account (name and phone number), your PIN (as a salted hash), parent and child profiles, face templates (encrypted), protected apps, policies, schedules, screen-time limits and usage, eye-safety configuration, the activity log, and settings. There is no QALQON server, so none of this is held by us.

2. Delete in the app (recommended)

  1. Open QALQON and unlock the parent controls.
  2. Go to Settings → Privacy.
  3. Tap Delete all data and confirm.

This permanently deletes the account, parent and child profiles, face templates, protected apps and policies, schedules, screen-time data, the activity log, requests and notifications, settings, session and lockout state, and the Android Keystore encryption key. It is a real deletion, not a deactivation. Uninstalling QALQON also removes its local data.

3. Request deletion

If you cannot use the in-app option, contact us at alisheraxmedov0012@gmail.com and we will help you complete the deletion. Because the data lives only on your device, we cannot delete it remotely; we will guide you through the on-device deletion or uninstall.

4. Retention

No QALQON-held data is retained after deletion, because QALQON does not hold a copy. On your device, the data is removed by the steps above.

5. Subscription

Deleting your QALQON account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel the subscription separately in Google Play (Play Store → Subscriptions).

6. Contact

Questions about deletion: alisheraxmedov0012@gmail.com.
