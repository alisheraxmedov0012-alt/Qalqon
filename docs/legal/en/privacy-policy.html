Privacy Policy

QALQON · v1.0 · Effective date: 7 October 2026

QALQON ("the app", "we") is an on-device parental-control application for Android (package uz.faceguard.app). This policy explains what data the app processes, where it is stored, how long it is kept and how it is deleted. Contact: [[SUPPORT_EMAIL]].

1. Introduction

QALQON is privacy-first and offline-first by design. It runs entirely on your device, has no Internet permission, and does not operate a user account server. This policy applies to the QALQON Android app and to the QALQON legal and support pages. It is written to match the app's actual behaviour (verified against the code); it is not a marketing claim and not legal advice.

2. Summary

  • QALQON works fully offline and cannot transmit your data over the network.
  • All data (parent profile, child profiles, face templates, settings, activity) is stored only on your device.
  • Face processing happens entirely on the device; face templates are stored encrypted and are never transmitted off the device.
  • There are no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • You can delete your account and all associated data in the app at any time, and request deletion externally.

4. Face and biometric-related data

  • The camera is used only to detect a face and compute a face template (an embedding) for parent/child recognition.
  • Raw camera frames are processed in memory and are not written to disk or to the media gallery.
  • A face template is stored only on your device and is encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore (device-bound). It is never sent to us or to any third party.
  • You can delete a parent or child face template at any time; deleting the account deletes the templates and the encryption key.
  • Child face data is enrolled and controlled by the parent/guardian; the child has no account of their own.

The legal classification of "biometric data" differs between jurisdictions. This section describes the technical facts of how face data is handled; it does not assert a particular legal classification.

5. Local (on-device) processing

"On-device" means face detection, embedding and matching run locally using models bundled in the app (ML Kit face detection and a TensorFlow Lite face-recognition model). Because the app has no Internet permission, no face or other personal data can be transmitted off the device. If a future version were to add networking, this policy would be updated before that version is released.

6. Encryption of stored face templates

Stored face templates are protected with AES-256-GCM using a key generated and held in the Android Keystore. A fresh random nonce is used for every encryption, the ciphertext is authenticated, and there is no plaintext fallback: a decryption failure is treated as an integrity problem, not silently accepted. We do not claim this is unbreakable; it is a standard, well-understood protection.

7. Storage

  • Biometric-related templates: stored encrypted at rest (see section 6).
  • Other app data (account, profiles, protected apps, schedules, screen-time data, activity log, settings): stored in the app's private storage (a Room database and DataStore). This data is not additionally encrypted by QALQON; it relies on the Android app sandbox and the device's own storage encryption.

8. Retention

  • Face templates are retained until you delete the face, the child, or the account.
  • Account and profile data are retained until you delete the account.
  • Child profiles are retained until you delete the child or the account.
  • Settings and configuration are retained until you change or delete them.
  • The local subscription cache is retained until you sign out or delete the account; the purchase itself is held by Google Play.
  • The activity log is retained until you clear it or delete the account.
  • Raw camera frames are processed in memory and are not retained as stored media.

QALQON performs no automatic inactivity deletion; data is kept until you delete it.

9. Deletion

In-app. Settings → Privacy → delete all data (with a confirmation). This removes the account, parent and child profiles, face templates, protected apps and policies, schedules, screen-time data, the activity log, requests and notifications, plus settings, session and lockout state, and it deletes the Android Keystore encryption key. This is a real deletion, not a deactivation.

External request. A public account-deletion page is provided at the official QALQON domain (/delete-account) explaining the deletion path and how to request assistance. Because QALQON stores your data only on your device and there is no server copy, deletion is completed on the device (in-app or by uninstalling the app).

Deleting your QALQON account does not automatically cancel a Google Play subscription. Manage or cancel the subscription separately in Google Play.

10. Permissions and why they are needed

  • Camera — to detect a face for recognition.
  • Usage Access — to know which app is in the foreground, so a protected app can be detected (protection only).
  • Display over other apps (overlay) — to show the blocking screen over a protected app.
  • Accessibility service — to detect protected-app window transitions and draw the touchable blocking overlay (see section 11).
  • Foreground service — to keep protection (and the camera session) running while QALQON is not on screen.
  • Notifications — to show the ongoing protection status and parent alerts.
  • Receive boot completed — to restore protection after a restart.
  • Biometric/fingerprint (OS) — for the optional system unlock of the parent UI.

11. Accessibility service

QALQON uses an Android Accessibility service as part of its protection enforcement, not as a utility or accessibility tool for the user. Enabling it requires a clear in-app disclosure and your explicit consent. The service reads only the foreground package name; it is configured with canRetrieveWindowContent=false, so screen text, passwords and notifications are never read. You can disable the service at any time in Android Settings.

12. Usage Access

Usage Access is used only to determine which app is currently in the foreground so that a protected app can be detected. QALQON does not collect your app content, and it does not send usage information to any server (there is no server).

13. Third-party components

  • Google Play Billing — processes the optional Premium subscription through Google Play.
  • AndroidX / Jetpack (Compose, Navigation, Lifecycle, Room, DataStore, Hilt) — application framework; they do not collect or transmit your data.
  • CameraX — camera capture on the device.
  • ML Kit face detection — face detection; the models are bundled in the app and run on the device.
  • TensorFlow Lite — runs the on-device face-recognition model.

QALQON contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. The app's build removes the network permissions that the ML Kit transport dependency injects through manifest merging, so the shipped app keeps no Internet permission.

14. Billing

QALQON Premium is sold as a Google Play subscription (a monthly plan with a 3-day free trial). Payment is processed by Google Play under Google's terms; QALQON does not collect or store card or payment details. QALQON stores only a local subscription status cache, which it re-verifies against Google Play. Purchases can be restored through Google Play. Google Play's own data practices are governed by Google's privacy policy, separate from QALQON's.

15. Security

Controls in place include: encrypted face templates (AES-256-GCM, Android Keystore); raw camera frames are never persisted; the PIN is stored only as a salted hash; no unnecessary network capability; release builds are minified/obfuscated; developer-only screens are excluded from release builds; sensitive values are not written to logs. We do not describe these as "military grade" or "100% secure".

16. Backup

Android backup and device-to-device transfer of app data are disabled (allowBackup="false" plus data-extraction rules that exclude every data domain from both cloud backup and device transfer), so QALQON's data is not copied to another device. The face-template encryption key is device-bound and is not included in any transfer.

17. Children's privacy

QALQON is a parental-control tool used by a parent or guardian. There are no child accounts. A child's profile and face template are created and controlled by the parent, stored on the device, and deleted when the parent deletes them or the account. The app is not directed to children and is not listed as a children's app.

18. Data sharing

QALQON does not share personal or sensitive user data with third parties. The only external interaction is Google Play Billing, which processes the subscription purchase through Google Play.

19. International users

Because processing happens on your device, your data generally stays on your device and is not transferred internationally by QALQON. Where you purchase a subscription, Google Play may process purchase-related data under Google's terms and in accordance with Google's infrastructure. The privacy rights available to you depend on your jurisdiction; to exercise a request, contact us. This policy does not claim certification under any specific privacy regulation.

20. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; the version and effective date above will change. Material changes will be reflected in the app or its listing.
