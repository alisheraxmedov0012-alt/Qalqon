Support

QALQON · v1.0 · Effective date: 7 October 2026

This page explains how to get help with QALQON.

1. In-app help (no network needed)

QALQON includes an offline Help Center with a searchable knowledge base, FAQs and troubleshooting. Open it from Settings → Support.

2. Contact us

Email: alisheraxmedov0012@gmail.com. We aim to respond within a few business days.

3. Common topics

  • Setup: parent profile, adding a child, protecting apps.
  • Permissions: camera, Usage Access, Accessibility, overlay, notifications.
  • Protection not working, or a capability is missing.
  • Camera failure and recovery.
  • Subscription, restore and cancellation (Google Play).
  • Account and data deletion.
  • Privacy questions.

4. Before you write

It helps to include your app version, Android version, device manufacturer/model and a short description of what happened. Please do not send face images or other sensitive data.
