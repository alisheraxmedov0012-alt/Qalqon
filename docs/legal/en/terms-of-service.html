1. The service

QALQON is a parental-control / child-protection app. It helps a parent or guardian limit and monitor a child's device use on the same device, using face recognition, app rules, schedules, screen-time limits and eye-safety features. QALQON runs on the device and works offline. Core protection (app blocking, recognition, PIN) is provided; Premium adds advanced parental controls (multiple child profiles, schedules, screen-time limits, eye safety, extra-time requests, and activity history).
