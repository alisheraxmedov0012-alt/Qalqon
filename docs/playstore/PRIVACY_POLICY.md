**QALQON — Privacy Policy**

_Last updated: [DATE]_

Qalqon ("the app", "we") is an on-device parental-control application for Android
(package `uz.faceguard.app`). This policy explains what data the app handles and how.
Contact: `[SUPPORT EMAIL]`. Developer: `[DEVELOPER / LEGAL ENTITY, ADDRESS]`.

## 1. Summary
- Qalqon works **fully offline**. It has **no Internet permission** and cannot transmit
  your data over the network.
- All data (parent profile, child profiles, face templates, settings, activity log) is
  stored **only on your device**.
- Face processing happens **entirely on the device**. Face templates are **never
  transmitted off the device** and are stored **encrypted**.
- You can **delete your account and all associated data in the app at any time** (and
  request deletion via a web resource — see §9).

## 2. Data we handle and why
| Data | Source | Stored | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Parent name, phone number | You type it at registration | Device-local database (Room) | Identify the account and the parent |
| PIN | You set it | Device-local, only as a salted PBKDF2 hash (never the raw PIN) | Unlock the parent controls |
| Camera frames | Your device camera | **Memory only, transient (per frame)** | Detect a face to recognise parent/child |
| Face template (embedding) | Derived on-device from a detected face | Device-local database, **encrypted (AES-256-GCM, Android Keystore)** | Recognise the enrolled parent/child face |
| Child profile (name, restriction level) | You type it | Device-local database | Apply parental rules to the child |
| Protected-app selection | You choose apps | Device-local database | Decide which apps are protected |
| Foreground app (package name) | Usage Access / Accessibility service | Memory / device-local logs | Detect when a protected app is open |
| Activity log (protection events) | Generated by the app | Device-local database | Show the parent what happened |
| Settings (limits, schedules, eye-safety) | You configure them | Device-local DataStore | Apply your rules |
| Subscription status | Google Play Billing | Device-local cache | Show your plan; Google Play holds the purchase |

## 3. Face and biometric data (children and parents)
- The camera is used only to detect a face and compute a **face template** (an
  embedding) for parent/child recognition. **Raw camera frames are processed in memory
  and are not written to disk.**
- A face template is stored **only on your device**, **encrypted** with a key held in
  the Android Keystore (device-bound). It is **never sent to us or any third party**.
- You can delete a parent or child face template at any time; deleting the account
  deletes the templates and the encryption key.
- Child face data is enrolled and controlled by the **parent/guardian**; the child has
  no account of their own.

## 4. On-device processing — not transmitted off-device
"On-device" means face detection, embedding and matching run locally using models
bundled in the app (ML Kit face detection; TensorFlow Lite MobileFaceNet). Because the
app has no Internet permission, **no face or other personal data is transmitted off the
device**.

## 5. Permissions and why they are needed
- **Camera** — to detect a face for recognition.
- **Usage Access (Usage Stats)** — to know which app is in the foreground, so a
  protected app can be detected. Used only for protection.
- **Display over other apps (Overlay)** — to show the blocking screen over a protected
  app (visual fallback).
- **Accessibility service** — to detect protected-app window transitions and to draw the
  touchable blocking overlay. The service reads **only the foreground package name**; it
  is configured with `canRetrieveWindowContent=false`, so **screen text, passwords and
  notifications are never read**. Enabling it requires a clear in-app disclosure and your
  explicit consent.
- **Notifications** — to show the ongoing protection status and parent alerts.
- **Receive boot completed** — to restore protection after a restart.
- **Biometric/Fingerprint** — for the optional OS unlock of the parent UI.

## 6. Data sharing
**None.** The app does not share personal or sensitive user data with third parties. It
contains no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs. The only external
interaction is **Google Play Billing**, which processes the subscription purchase through
Google Play under Google's terms.

## 7. Children's privacy
Qalqon is a parental-control tool **used by a parent/guardian**. There are no child
accounts. A child's profile and face template are created and controlled by the parent,
stored on the device, and deleted when the parent deletes them or the account. The app is
**not directed to children** and is not listed as a children's / "Designed for Families"
app.

## 8. Security
Face templates are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM using a device-bound Android
Keystore key. The PIN is stored only as a salted PBKDF2 hash. Backup and device-to-device
transfer of app data are disabled.

## 9. Your choices and deletion
- **Delete data in-app:** Settings → Privacy → delete all data. This removes the account,
  all profiles, face templates, settings and logs, and deletes the encryption key.
- **External deletion request:** `[ACCOUNT DELETION WEB URL]` (see
  `docs/playstore/ACCOUNT_DELETION.md`). Because there is no server, this resource
  explains the on-device deletion path and how to request help.

## 10. Changes to this policy

update means you accept the revised policy.
