## In-app help (already implemented)
QALQON ships an offline Help Center (`feature/help`) with a local knowledge base, search,
FAQs and troubleshooting, plus a QALQON Assistant entry. The Settings → Support category
links to it. It requires no network.

## Support topics the help should cover
- Setup (parent profile, child, protected apps)
- Permissions (camera, Usage Access, Accessibility, overlay, notifications)
- Protection not working / degraded capability
- Camera failure and recovery
- Subscription, restore and cancellation
- Account/data deletion
- Privacy questions

Most of these are addressed by the in-app Help Center and the Stage 6/8 guidance. The
external contact channels above are the remaining manual items.
