- **Free trial** — 3 days, then the paid monthly subscription at the price displayed at purchase.
- **Billing & price** — processed by Google Play; the price is what Google Play displays (never
  hardcoded); automatic monthly renewal.
- **Cancellation** — through Google Play subscriptions; cancellation ≠ account deletion.
- **Refunds** — governed by Google Play purchase policies; no absolute "no refunds" clause.
- **Technical limitations** — permissions/OEM/battery/background dependencies; recognition and

- **Devices & OEMs** — behavior varies by manufacturer/model; best-effort compatibility.
- **Acceptable use** — lawful parental-control use only; no illegal surveillance or abuse.
- **Account deletion** — in-app and external; data deletion ≠ subscription cancellation.
